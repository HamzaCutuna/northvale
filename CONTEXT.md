
## Custom booking calendar
Check-in and check-out now use an inline custom calendar in the booking card, with ivory and pine styling, a serif month title, subtle today indicator and selected/range styling. Date buttons replace native browser date inputs; hidden values preserve form submission. Check-in advances to check-out, invalid earlier departures clear when arrival changes, and past dates are disabled. Keyboard arrows, Home/End, Page Up/Down and Escape are supported. Booking submission still validates missing or invalid dates and generates the same local stay summary.
